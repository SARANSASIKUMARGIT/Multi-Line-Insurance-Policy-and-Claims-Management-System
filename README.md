PROJECT TITLE : Multi-Line Insurance Policy and Claims Management System

PROBLEM STATEMENT : 
  1. Problem Statement
An insurance carrier, which manages vehicle, property, and life insurance, currently uses a legacy system that results in manual, slow, and inconsistent policy quoting, issuance, and claims handling. The lack of a unified platform makes cross-selling difficult, and the manual claims review process leads to long resolution times, high operational costs, and poor customer experience.
The business needs a single Salesforce solution to:
  1.1 Standardize and accelerate the quoting and policy issuance process for all insurance lines.
  1.2 Automate claim initiation and routing based on policy type and claim severity.
  1.3 Provide a 360-degree view of the Customer, Policy, and Claim data in one place.

2. Objectives
  2.1 Design a flexible data model capable of storing diverse product data (e.g., vehicle VIN for Vehicle, square footage for Property).
  2.2 Implement Screen Flows to guide agents through complex, multi-step quoting processes for different policy types.
  2.3 Develop a dynamic Apex/LWC Claims Dashboard for adjusters to efficiently manage their workload.
  2.4 Automate claims approval/escalation based on claim amount and policy history using Record-Triggered Flows.
  2.5 Enforce data validation across all insurance products to ensure compliance and accuracy.
